Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

ABDULREGULAR Agency ("we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how information submitted through the contact form on our website is handled.

1. Information we collect

When you use our contact form, we ask for your name, email address and message. Please do not include passwords, payment card details or other sensitive personal information in the message.

2. Why we use your information

We use the information you submit to review your enquiry, respond to you, discuss a possible project and keep a record of the conversation where reasonably necessary.

3. Where information is stored

Contact form submissions are stored in our Supabase-hosted database. Access to submitted messages is restricted through database access policies to authorized agency administration. Our website hosting and database providers may process information as needed to provide their services.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your contact form information. We do not share it for third-party advertising. Information may be processed by the service providers that host and operate our website, or disclosed where required by applicable law.

5. Retention and security

We keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage the business relationship, maintain appropriate records or meet legal requirements. We use access controls intended to protect submitted messages, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about the personal information you submitted, request correction, or ask for deletion where applicable. We may need to verify your request and may retain information where a lawful reason requires it.

7. External links

Our website may link to services such as WhatsApp or email. Those services have their own privacy practices, which are outside this policy.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its update date.

9. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email abdulregularofficial@gmail.com.
